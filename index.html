Press Play Daily

Press Play Daily is a personal project that publishes gaming videos to its own YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy

This app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel owned by the app's creator. It does not collect, store, share or sell personal data of any other user.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The creator can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google account security settings.
